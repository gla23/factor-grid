## Expert extensions!

Start at 1, then count up from 1 to 10 (skipping 7). Look at the shape that is created as you follow the pattern a few times. How fast can you do it?

Now follow the same pattern, but start at 3. This is the 3 times table! Try this for other numbers like 4,5,6,7,8,9,12 etc.

Can you learn the numbers on the graph and imagine them in your head? The coloured lines are added to help with this. Perhaps use the major system (or other number-image mapping) to and make the images interact with each other, and the lines, so they are more sticky.
